    How the value is worked out

    implied value = curve(TVL) × spike discount × 0.60 Bear case × 0.50 · bull case × 0.70 · the range on each row runs from bear to bull.

    The curve is fitted fresh on every run, by ordinary least squares on the log of market cap against the log of TVL, over the 128 tokenised peers in the snapshot: log(mcap) = 6.9646 + 0.5223 · log(TVL), R² 0.1939, residual RMSE 2.0771 in log space. A slope well under one means the market pays a smaller multiple for each extra dollar locked as protocols grow — the multiple falls as TVL rises:

    The spike discount reads a protocol's seven-day move against its own category. Take the protocol's 7-day TVL change, subtract its category's median change, clamp the gap to −30…+60, and discount by exp(−2 · gap / 100). A protocol that spiked ahead of its category is marked down; one that fell behind it is marked up, on the peer-validated finding that weekly moves partly reverse. The discount spans 0.30× to 1.82×.

The free regression's own slope on the gap is −6.24, and at that strength the board starts promoting protocols on the strength of a crash alone: at k = −3 the top ten already changes. k = −2 is one third of the free slope, beats k = −1 on both tests, and leaves the top five exactly where they were.

    Six things to know

    1. The value is derived from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, the seven-day change, and the listing age — and never from a hand-written list of names.
    2. The spike term is mean reversion the peers support, not a forecast that a fall will keep falling or a spike will keep spiking.
    3. The ×0.60 is a judgement haircut for the risk that a launch never happens or never pays, not a fitted number: the peers calibrate the curve, not the launch risk.
    4. No launch-odds term is applied. A logistic model of whether a protocol has a token barely beat guessing — log-loss 0.6842 against a 0.6884 base rate, AUC 0.608 — so it adds noise, not signal.
    5. Every farm plan is sized by the protocol's TVL, prices gas per chain, and holds 30 to 60 days by age. Eleven rows carry a step to wait for a spike to settle; five carry a step to enter while the move is fresh.
    6. What the model does not do: it does not predict whether or when any protocol issues a token. An implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record held — nothing more.
